title: "Better handling of links to other sites"
---
We render our product docs with Python-Markdown and readers keep complaining that when they click a link to another website they lose their place in the docs. Other tools we've tried seem to handle external links better out of the box.

Could Python-Markdown do something smarter with external links? I'm not sure what the best approach is, just that the current behaviour isn't great for documentation sites.
